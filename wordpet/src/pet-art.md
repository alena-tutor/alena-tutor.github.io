# WordPet pet illustrations

Created with the built-in image_gen tool. The existing fox-room artwork was the edit target. The three new images preserve the room, cushion, bowl, lighting and framing and replace the central animal.

Assets: cat-room.base64, dog-room.base64, dragon-room.base64. The generated PNG originals were copied into the project as JPEG data encoded in base64, resized to 900 px wide at quality 78 without changing composition.

## Prompt template

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: main pet-room illustration for the WordPet learning app. Input image is the edit target. Replace only the large central fox sitting on the green cushion with SUBJECT. Preserve the cozy sunlit room, bookshelf, window, green round cushion, orange pet bowl, camera position, 3:2 landscape composition, warm terracotta and sage palette, photorealistic soft-detail style, shadows and lighting. The new pet sits in the same position and fits fully inside the frame. One central pet only, no people, no clothing, no text, no UI, no watermark. Keep all background furnishings unchanged. Final image should be coherent, polished and welcoming.
