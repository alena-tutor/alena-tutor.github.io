# WordPet pet illustrations

## Current assets (1.10.0)

Five pets: fox, silver tabby kitten, golden retriever puppy, mint-green baby dragon and pink piglet. Every pet has sad, calm and happy portraits. Generated with the built-in image_gen tool from the four original WordPet room illustrations as identity and style references.

The generated sprite atlas was visually checked: five species rows, sad/calm/happy columns. Actual seams were measured before extracting tiles (x: 0,388,774,1190; y: 0,265,529,793,1057,1322). Only tile extraction and JPEG encoding were done locally; character expressions were generated by image_gen. A one-pixel inset removes neighbouring tile seams. Original room furnishings, sage cushion, terracotta bowl and warm lighting remain consistent.

Saved project paths:

- src/fox-room.base64, src/cat-room.base64, src/dog-room.base64, src/dragon-room.base64, src/pig-room.base64: calm portraits, JPEG quality 88.
- src/pet-moods.json: sad and happy JPEG portraits for all five species, base64 encoded.

The build embeds shared artwork into the application once. No per-pupil illustration files are created. Runtime mood is computed from food and cleanliness; no additional image service is called during play.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: one production game sprite atlas for WordPet, a vocabulary pet game. Input images 1–4 are identity, room and style references: fox, silver tabby kitten, golden retriever puppy, mint-green baby dragon. Make ONE seamless rectangular sprite sheet, exactly THREE equal columns and FIVE equal rows, 15 panels total, with NO gutters, borders, text or labels. Each individual panel must have a 3:2 landscape aspect ratio, so the whole sheet has a 9:10 portrait aspect ratio. Request high resolution around 3072x3410 so faces stay detailed. Every panel is a standalone full-room composition matching the references: bookshelf left, sunny window right, sage green round floor cushion, orange pet bowl at bottom right. Keep camera, furniture, cushion, lighting, scale and framing consistent across all panels. ROW 1: same orange fox from image1. ROW 2: same silver tabby kitten from image2. ROW 3: same golden puppy from image3. ROW 4: same green baby dragon from image4. ROW 5: NEW very cute small pink piglet, soft natural pink skin with tiny pale bristles, floppy ears, small round snout, anatomically correct four legs with cloven hooves and a tiny curly tail, matching the soft realistic illustrated style of the references. In EVERY ROW, LEFT panel is SAD: clearly downcast eyes, drooped ears or head, subdued slumped seated pose, mouth closed; gentle sadness, safe and healthy, no tears, injury or distress. MIDDLE panel is CALM: upright seated relaxed neutral interested expression, looking at viewer. RIGHT panel is HAPPY: lifted head and ears, visibly cheerful bright eyes, relaxed playful open mouth or believable gentle smile, more lively seated posture. Expressions must be clearly distinguishable in thumbnail size, while preserving that row's exact identity and markings. All animals fully visible, centered on same cushion, one main animal per panel, enough breathing room around head/feet/tail. Photorealistic soft-detail, warm terracotta and sage interior, polished original reference rendering. NO UI, NO words, NO watermarks, NO extra animals, NO spiders, NO props added, NO warped anatomy. Important atlas order: fox/kitten/puppy/dragon/piglet top-to-bottom; sad/calm/happy left-to-right. Exactly 5 rows and 3 columns, no merged panels.
